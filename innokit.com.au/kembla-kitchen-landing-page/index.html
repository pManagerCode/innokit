Innokit incorporates Kembla Kitchens, our second showroom, recently purchased in Port Kembla.

With over 20 years of exposure and heritage, the well established Kembla Kitchens business provides Innokit a trusted frontage and showroom in the Wollongong and South Coast area.

Visit us if you’re looking for tangible inspiration or just to ask questions as you embark on your next kitchen renovation or larger joinery project. Showroom visits are by appointment only so please call or email us to arrange your appointment.
